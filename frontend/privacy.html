Blaiz Business Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026
Privacy Policy version: 1.0

Blaiz Business Manager respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information associated with your account and business.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Blaiz Business Manager may collect, how that information is used, how it may be stored or processed, and the choices available to you.

By using Blaiz Business Manager, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Blaiz Business Manager, we may collect and process the following categories of information.

2. Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your account credentials.
  • Your account type and role.

Passwords are handled through the application's authentication system and are not intended to be stored as plain-text passwords.

3. Store Information

When a store is created, we may collect information provided by the store owner, including:

  • Store name.
  • Store phone number.
  • Store email address.
  • Store address.
  • Business type.

4. Worker Information

Store owners may create worker accounts for authorized employees or other people working with the store.

Information associated with worker accounts may include their name, login credentials, role, account status and association with the relevant store.

5. Business Information

Blaiz Business Manager allows users to enter and manage information relating to their business.

This may include:

  • Product information.
  • Inventory information.
  • Customer information.
  • Sales records.
  • Prices and quantities.
  • Discount information.
  • Customer balances or debts.
  • Receipt information.
  • Receipt numbers.
  • Worker activity associated with the store.

Users are responsible for ensuring that information they enter into the application is accurate and that they have the appropriate authority to provide any personal information relating to other individuals.

6. Payment and Subscription Information

Blaiz Business Manager may provide premium subscription services.

When you purchase a premium subscription, information relating to the transaction may be processed, including payment references, transaction identifiers, subscription status, subscription dates, selected plan information and other information necessary to confirm the transaction.

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Paystack. Payment card or other payment credentials are handled by the applicable payment provider according to its own privacy practices.

7. Email and Verification Information

Blaiz Business Manager may use email services to provide account-related communications and verification or password-reset functionality.

Where applicable, information such as email addresses, verification codes and password-reset information may be processed for these purposes.

8. Terms and Privacy Consent Information

When an owner creates an account, Blaiz Business Manager records the user's acceptance of the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

This consent record may include:

  • Whether the Terms of Use were accepted.
  • The date and time of acceptance.
  • The accepted Terms of Use version.
  • The accepted Privacy Policy version.

This information is used to maintain a record of the agreement associated with the account.

9. How We Use Information

Information collected through Blaiz Business Manager may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide business management features.
  • Manage products and inventory.
  • Manage customers and sales.
  • Generate and verify receipts.
  • Manage authorized workers.
  • Process and verify premium subscriptions.
  • Provide password-reset and verification services.
  • Maintain application security.
  • Detect and prevent unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain and improve the service.
  • Provide important service-related communications.
  • Maintain records of legal and privacy consent.

10. Receipt Verification

Blaiz Business Manager may provide a receipt verification feature that allows an authorized store owner or worker to verify a receipt number belonging to their store.

Receipt verification may return information associated with the matching receipt so that the authorized user can confirm its authenticity and details.

A store's receipt information is intended to remain associated with that store and should not be accessed without authorization.

11. Notifications and Activity Information

Blaiz Business Manager may maintain information about activities performed within a store in order to provide notifications and activity-related features.

Depending on the feature, this may include activities such as sales, inventory changes, customer changes, product changes, receipt verification and other relevant business activities.

12. How We Store Information

Blaiz Business Manager uses online infrastructure and database services to store and process application data.

Business and account information may be stored on servers operated by third-party infrastructure or database providers used by Blaiz Business Manager.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

13. Third-Party Service Providers

Blaiz Business Manager may use third-party service providers to operate certain parts of the service.

These may include providers for:

  • Cloud hosting and application infrastructure.
  • Database storage.
  • Payment processing.
  • Email delivery.
  • Authentication and related technical services.

Third-party providers may process information as necessary to provide the services they supply to Blaiz Business Manager.

14. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Blaiz Business Manager does not intentionally request or require access to information such as your device contacts, microphone or precise GPS location for the ordinary operation of the business management features described in this Privacy Policy.

If future features require additional categories of information, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

15. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate Blaiz Business Manager, process payments, deliver emails, maintain infrastructure, protect the service or comply with legal obligations.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, lawful government requests or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the service.

16. Store Owner Responsibility

Store owners are responsible for the business and customer information they enter into Blaiz Business Manager.

Store owners should only enter or provide access to information where they have a lawful and appropriate basis for doing so.

Store owners are also responsible for managing worker access appropriately and removing access when a worker is no longer authorized to use the store account.

17. Data Retention

We may retain account and business information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or protect the service.

When information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements and our operational procedures.

18. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account or associated information through the available account or support channels.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for legitimate business records, required to resolve disputes, or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

19. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion or other appropriate handling of your information.

blaizbusinessmanager@gmail.com

20. Children's Privacy

Blaiz Business Manager is intended for people who are legally able to enter into the applicable agreements and use the service for legitimate business purposes.

We do not intentionally design the service to collect personal information from children.

21. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through Blaiz Business Manager.

These measures may include authentication controls, password protection, access restrictions and secure communication with application services where supported.

Users are also responsible for protecting their passwords and account credentials.

22. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Blaiz Business Manager, applicable requirements, third-party services or our privacy practices.

When material changes are made, users may be notified or required to review the updated Privacy Policy where appropriate.
